Update #s1 — Serious Criminality sentence threshold: ≥6 months → >6 months

2026-08-20  ·  Section 3.2 — Criminal Inadmissibility  ·  ✅ Applied  ·  Reported by: Raymond Anya

The term Serious Criminality is used in two distinct contexts in IRPA, and the sentence threshold is very subtly different in each:

Serious Criminality — Threshold by Context
Context Provision Sentence threshold (in Canada) Meaning
Inadmissibility IRPA s. 36(1)(a) > 6 months A sentence of exactly 6 months does NOT trigger inadmissibility for serious criminality
Loss of IAD appeal right IRPA s. 64(2) ≥ 6 months A sentence of exactly 6 months DOES strip the IAD appeal right

The key takeaway: Parliament deliberately drew the line at different points. A sentence of exactly 6 months sits in the gap — the person is not inadmissible for serious criminality under IRPA s. 36(1)(a), but if they are inadmissible through the other limb (max ≥ 10 years), they also lose their IAD appeal right under IRPA s. 64(2).

Serious Criminality — Scenario Examples
Situation Inadmissible for Serious Criminality? (IRPA s. 36(1)(a)) Has IAD Appeal Right? (IRPA s. 64(2))
Max ≥10 yrs, actual sentence 3 months ✅ Yes (max ≥10 yrs limb) ✅ Yes (sentence <6 months, s.64 not triggered)
Max ≥10 yrs, actual sentence 6 months exactly ✅ Yes (max ≥10 yrs limb) ❌ No (≥6 months, s.64 strips appeal)
Max ≥10 yrs, actual sentence 7 months ✅ Yes (both limbs) ❌ No (≥6 months, s.64 strips appeal)
Max <10 yrs, actual sentence 3 months ❌ No (neither limb met) N/A (s.64 not engaged)
Max <10 yrs, actual sentence 6 months exactly ❌ No (neither limb met) N/A (s.64 not engaged)
Max <10 yrs, actual sentence 7 months ✅ Yes (>6 months limb) ❌ No (≥6 months, s.64 strips appeal)

Update #s2 — Deemed Rehabilitation waiting period for hybrid offences clarified

2026-08-22  ·  Section 3.2 — Criminal Inadmissibility  ·  ✅ Applied  ·  Reported by: Editorial review

The cheatsheet stated the Deemed Rehabilitation waiting period for a single hybrid offence as "5 years after sentence completion." This is correct only when the offence carries a maximum penalty of less than 10 years in Canada. The section has been updated to make this condition explicit.

Deemed Rehabilitation — Waiting Period (Outside Canada, Single Offence)
Offence type Condition Waiting period
Single non-serious offence Canadian equivalent max < 10 years 10 years after sentence completion
Single hybrid offence Canadian equivalent max < 10 years 10 years after sentence completion
Single hybrid offence Canadian equivalent max ≥ 10 years → Serious Criminality ❌ Deemed Rehabilitation not available

Source: IRPR s. 18
